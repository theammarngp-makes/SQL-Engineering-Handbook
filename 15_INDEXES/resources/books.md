# Books

Recommended books for going deeper on database internals and indexing
than this module can cover in full.

- **Database Internals** — Alex Petrov. Covers B-Tree implementations,
  storage engines, and distributed systems fundamentals at an
  engineering-implementation level.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** — Martin Kleppmann. Chapter 3
  ("Storage and Retrieval") directly covers B-Trees, LSM-Trees, and
  indexing trade-offs; the rest of the book is essential context for how
  indexing decisions fit into larger system design.
- **SQL Performance Explained** — Markus Winand. Entirely dedicated to
  indexing across MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, and Oracle — the closest
  single-book match to this module's scope.
- **High Performance MySQL** — Silvia Botros, Jeremy Tinley, Baron
  Schwartz. MySQL/InnoDB-specific, with deep coverage of indexing,
  replication, and operational tuning.
- **PostgreSQL 14 Internals** — Egor Rogov. Free online, PostgreSQL-
  specific coverage of index structures (B-Tree, GiST, GIN, BRIN) at an
  implementation level.
