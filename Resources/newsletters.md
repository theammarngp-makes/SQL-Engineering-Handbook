# 📬 Newsletters

*Part of the [Resources Library](README.md) — [SQL Engineering Handbook](../README.md)*

Second-wave file — these seven files (`newsletters.md` through `awesome-tools.md`) weren't given the full documentation-team treatment in the original spec, just a one-line description each. This uses a lighter card format on purpose: a scannable table instead of a full [Resource Card Template](README.md#resource-card-template) per entry. If a specific entry needs the deeper treatment later, it can graduate to the fuller format.

---

| Newsletter | What It Is | Best For | Free / Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Postgres Weekly** | Weekly digest of PostgreSQL news, releases, and community articles | Staying current on PostgreSQL specifically without manually checking release notes | Free |
| **TLDR Data** | Daily digest covering data engineering, analytics, and AI/data tooling news in a few-minutes read | A fast daily pulse on the wider data ecosystem, not just SQL | Free |
| **Data Engineering Weekly** | Curated weekly roundup of data engineering articles, tools, and industry moves | Data engineers wanting a filtered signal from a very noisy space | Free |
| **dbt Labs Newsletter** | Product updates, community highlights, and analytics-engineering practice content from dbt Labs | Anyone on the Analytics Engineer Roadmap who wants to stay current without checking the blog manually | Free |
| **SQLServerCentral Newsletter** | Long-running digest of SQL Server-specific articles, forum highlights, and scripts | Microsoft SQL Server / T-SQL practitioners specifically | Free |

> [!TIP]
> Subscribe to one or two, not all five. A newsletter you delete unread is worse than no newsletter — it just adds guilt without adding knowledge.
