# 💬 Communities

*Part of the [Resources Library](README.md) — [SQL Engineering Handbook](../README.md)*

Where to ask a question that a search engine can't quite answer, or to see how other people are actually debugging their own queries in real time.

---

| Community | Platform | Best For | Free / Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **r/SQL** | Reddit | General SQL questions, career discussion, casual troubleshooting | Free |
| **r/dataengineering** | Reddit | Broader data-platform and pipeline discussion, tool comparisons, career threads | Free |
| **dbt Community Slack** | Slack | Analytics engineering-specific discussion, direct access to dbt Labs staff and experienced practitioners | Free |
| **Database Administrators Stack Exchange** | Stack Exchange | Precise, well-moderated Q&A for specific technical database problems | Free |
| **PostgreSQL Mailing Lists** (`pgsql-general`) | Mailing list | Direct access to the PostgreSQL core community, including contributors | Free |

> [!NOTE]
> Read a community's pinned rules/FAQ before posting — SQL communities in particular have low tolerance for "please debug my entire query" posts with no attempt shown first.
